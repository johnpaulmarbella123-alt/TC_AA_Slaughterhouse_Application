# Admin Module: Illegal Slaughter Oversight

**Source File**: `admin_panel/lib/screens/illegal_slaughter_reports_view.dart`  
**Target Roles**: City Veterinary Enforcement Task Force, Meat Inspection Enforcement Officers.

---

## Purpose & Functionality
Provides an enforcement dashboard for monitoring, triaging, and responding to citizen whistleblower tips concerning illegal slaughtering, hot meat ("botcha") distribution, and sanitary violations.

---

## Investigation Lifecycle
- **Mobile Submissions**: Unreviewed reports submitted by citizens containing incident descriptions, addresses, and photographic evidence.
- **Under Investigation**: Assigned to veterinary inspectors for on-site physical surveillance and raid coordination with local law enforcement (PNP).
- **Resolved / Dismissed**: Enforcement action completed (e.g. *Meat Seizure*, *Citation Ticket Issued*, *False Alarm*).
