# PDF Generation & Document Lifecycle

Statutory compliance and government audit requirements demand rigorous record-keeping for every livestock transaction. The system produces three official legal documents:
1. **Daily Slaughterhouse Production Report** (Legal Size: 8.5" x 14")
2. **Official Meat Condemnation Slip** (Issued by City Veterinarian / Meat Inspector)
3. **Official Slaughter & Inspection Receipt** (With dynamic verification barcode)

## Condemnation Slip Data Pipeline

When an animal fails ante-mortem examination or a carcass is condemned post-mortem:
1. Meat Inspector inputs veterinary findings and selects condemnation severity (`Partial` or `Total`) in `slaughter_inspection_page.dart`.
2. The reason (e.g. *Swine Erysipelas*, *Septicemia*, *Localized Abscess*) is saved to `slaughter_inspection.condemnation_reason`.
3. The mobile client renders an official digital **Condemnation Notice & Slip** (`condemnation_slip_screen.dart`), detailing:
   - Certificate Reference Number (`CS-YYYY-XXX`)
   - Seizure & Incineration Order details
   - Statutory reference under the Meat Inspection Code of the Philippines (R.A. 9296)
